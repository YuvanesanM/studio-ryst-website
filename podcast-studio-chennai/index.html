FAQ

Podcast studio questions.

Do you provide microphones and audio equipment?

Yes — broadcast-grade microphones and audio gear are included in every booking. Bring your own camera for video, or add a camera operator/cinematographer as a paid add-on.

Is the podcast studio actually soundproofed?

The room is acoustically treated for broadcast-quality sound, keeping recordings clean even though we're located in busy T-Nagar.

How many people can record at once?

The sofa setup comfortably seats a host and 2-3 guests, which covers most interview and panel-format podcasts.

What's the minimum booking time for the podcast studio?

2 hours — long enough for a full episode with time to set up and wrap. 4 and 8-hour blocks are available for multi-episode recording days.

Can I record video for YouTube along with the audio?

Yes — the space has ambient, camera-ready lighting built in. Bring your own camera, or we can arrange a cinematographer for you as a paid add-on.

How do I book the podcast studio?

Use the Book Now page to pick a date and duration — it creates your booking instantly and hands you a prefilled WhatsApp message to send us. Prefer to talk first? Message us on WhatsApp directly.

Ready to record in Chennai's podcast studio?

Pick a date and duration — your price is confirmed instantly.
