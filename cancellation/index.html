Refund and Cancellation

Effective Date: February 1st, 2026

Studio RYST reserves time slots and resources for confirmed bookings, and enforces the following cancellation and refund policy for operational fairness.

1. Booking Confirmation

Bookings are confirmed only after full payment is received. Once confirmed, the time slot is exclusively reserved for you.

2. Cancellation Policy

  • More than 72 hours before the session: 90% refund of the booking amount, with 10% retained as an administrative fee.
  • Less than 72 hours before the session, or a no-show: no refund.

3. Rescheduling Policy

One rescheduling is allowed per booking, with at least 48 hours' advance notice. New dates are subject to availability. Rescheduling within 48 hours may incur extra charges. No-shows are not eligible for rescheduling.

4. Late Arrival & Overtime

Your booked time begins at the scheduled start time regardless of late arrival. Overtime is charged at our current hourly rates, subject to studio availability.

5. Studio-Initiated Cancellation

If the studio needs to cancel a confirmed booking, clients receive either a full refund or a free rescheduling. The studio is not responsible for any indirect expenses this may cause.

6. Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed within 7–10 business days to the original payment method.

7. Non-Refundable Situations

No refunds are issued for rule violations, misconduct, property damage, or early termination of a session caused by the client.

8. Special Event / Bulk Bookings

Separate terms may apply for full-day or event bookings — these are communicated at the time of confirmation.
