Good to know

What's the minimum booking?

Two hours for the podcast and photo studios, four for the kitchen. Four- and eight-hour blocks cost less per hour.

Is GST included in the rates?

No — GST is added at 18% on the taxable value. Your quote shows the exact breakup for every line, and a tax invoice follows your payment.

How do I confirm a slot?

Book online, see your total with GST, pay the full amount and the slot is yours at once. Prefer WhatsApp? Send the request and we'll reply with a payment link.

Can I reschedule?

Once, free, with at least 48 hours' notice. Within 48 hours of the slot there's no refund or reschedule, and payments are otherwise non-refundable.

Do you provide cameras?

Microphones, lights and the kitchen are included. Cameras, lenses and recorders aren't — bring your own, or add a cinematographer as a paid extra.
